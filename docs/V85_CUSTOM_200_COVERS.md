# VARAQ 8.5 — 200 unique concept covers integrated

The catalog includes a distinct, original concept design for each of the 200 titles in `data/books.json`.

- Books 001–010: cinematic custom illustrations.
- Books 011–200: bespoke category- and title-aware editorial vector art, with Persian title typesetting (author credit where present in data).
- Full images: `public/concept-art/book-NNN.webp` (480x672).
- Thumbnails: `public/concept-thumb/book-NNN.webp` (240x336).
- Catalog mapping: `data/CUSTOM_COVER_MANIFEST_V85.json`.
- Cache identifier: `?v=85` to avoid old immutable cached images.

These images are NOT official publisher / specific-edition covers, and do not verify the ISBN, title rights, permission to reuse publisher imagery, or paid PDF distribution rights. All existing rights and payment gates remain in place. Existing private data must be preserved when upgrading.
